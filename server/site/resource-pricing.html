No invented pricing

This site does not publish a universal transaction fee, free-live tier or settled billing schedule. Request a written quotation before making a commercial commitment. Sandbox availability does not establish a price or service guarantee for production use.

02

What to include

Tell us your countries, expected payment volume, provider receipt formats, number of receiving phones, billing platform and support needs. Include your preferred hosting arrangement and any data-location requirements. Share examples with personal details removed.

03

Provider charges

Mobile money transfer, withdrawal, account and network fees are determined by the provider and your receiving arrangement. They are separate from any gateway service agreement. The gateway does not calculate or guarantee those fees.

04

Get in touch

Contact contact@ispbillingpay.com with your integration brief. Pricing, invoicing, tax treatment, support commitments and cancellation terms must be confirmed in the applicable written order or agreement.
