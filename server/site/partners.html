A GOOD FIRST CONVERSATION

Start with the way
your customers pay.

You do not need a finished integration to get in touch. A short introduction with these details gives us a useful starting point.

  • Your business or platform, and the countries you serve.
  • The mobile money provider and receiving-account type you use.
  • Your billing platform and how it activates internet packages.
  • Your expected payment volume and current payment-support challenges.
  • Who will manage the receiving phone and help customers when a connection fails.

If we need receipt examples, remove customer names, phone numbers, balances and usable transaction references before sharing them.

Compatibility is the starting point.

Uganda and Ghana have built-in sender lists. Custom sender setup opens onboarding for other networks and countries allowed by the deployment, without payment-provider API keys. Validate genuine receipt samples before enabling activation; the current parser does not support every language or message layout.

Provider names do not imply a commercial partnership or endorsement. New markets need receipt-format validation and an agreed operational setup.
