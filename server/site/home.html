A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
get started.

Visit the help centre ↗
Do you collect or hold our customers' money?+

No. Customers pay your mobile money receiving account directly. The gateway handles receipt records, matching and notifications to your billing system.

Do I need a mobile money API contract?+

This connection uses an Android listener on your receiving phone, so it does not require a provider API integration. Your receiving account still needs to meet the provider's terms and limits. Your billing system uses a separate ISP Billing Pay merchant API key.

What happens if a payment cannot be matched?+

It remains unmatched with a reason for review. Your billing system can expose a claim flow or let an authorised operator assign the receipt to the correct purchase reference.

Will this connect a customer after their MAC changes?+

The gateway links payments to purchase references. Your billing system handles the username, remaining package, device binding and router login. The claim API can support that recovery without creating another payment.

What happens when the listener phone goes offline?+

The app queues payment messages it has received until they can be uploaded. The gateway exposes last-seen information. Keep the phone charged, connected and monitored; reconcile missed notifications against your receiving account.
