01

Direct payment today

Customers send money to the merchant’s receiving number. A dedicated Android listener forwards supported confirmation messages. The gateway records receipts, matches them to waiting purchases and sends signed events to the merchant’s billing system. The gateway does not receive or hold customer funds.

02

Network configuration

Built-in sender choices cover supported networks in Uganda and Ghana. Other markets may configure explicit custom sender names. Compatibility depends on the actual receipt format, language and receiving account. Validate genuine samples before enabling automated activation.

03

Provider APIs later

Direct provider integrations are planned and remain unavailable in the product. Their availability will depend on technical integration, provider approval and commercial arrangements. No provider name on this site implies a partnership or endorsement.

04

Keep channel identity

Treat a channel as part of your reconciliation context. Keep merchant, provider, receiving account and transaction identity together. Future integrations must preserve those boundaries instead of assuming that a transaction reference is globally unique.
